Principle:

Wind energy is converted into rotational mechanical energy when moving air strikes the blades. In a modern wind turbine, the rotating shaft can drive a generator to produce electrical energy.

🏺 Traditional vs Modern Wind Systems

🏺 Traditional Wind Systems

  • Used for grinding grains.
  • Used for pumping water.
  • Wind energy mainly produced mechanical motion.
  • Often used wooden blades.
  • Smaller structures.

⚡ Modern Wind Turbines

  • Use aerodynamic blades.
  • Can generate electricity.
  • Use a shaft and generator.
  • Can be very large in size.
  • Used in large wind farms.
